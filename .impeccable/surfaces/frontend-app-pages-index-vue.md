# Home (`/`, `/en`)

Mode: Persuade, with Experience ambition (it is the portfolio). Primary action: the contact form ("Cuéntame tu proyecto"). Primary search intent: "desarrollo web / software a medida Ibiza"; service queries have their own landings.

## Direction contract

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/hero-keynote-portrait.png` (round 2, option 3). Motion and section reference: `.impeccable/explore/hybrid/index.html`.

THESIS: A product keynote for one independent builder. Pablo presents his own work on a dark stage: him first, then each shipped product walks on and plays for real. Refuses the dark-dev-portfolio template (glass cards, stack logos, gradient text) and the cold luxury agency.

OWN-WORLD: Warm film black `#0C0B0A`, off-white `#EEEAE3` type, warm greys for secondary text, one tally red-orange `#FF4D2E` reserved for the primary action and live states. Heavy tight geometric grotesk display, fully rounded pills, generous air. Depth only from light: a warm key light on the portrait, screens glowing on black, soft floor reflections. No borders-as-decoration, no cards, no glass.

STORY: Understands: one developer in Ibiza designs, builds and ships webs, custom software and AI automations. Believes: real products recorded as they run today (Vestta, Post Producciones, Tierras de Ibiza, La Casa de Susana, Brisa), live data and a verifiable security response. Does: "Cuéntame tu proyecto". Headings chain into one story (origin, products, still running, built daily, unseen work, your project); never film-production vocabulary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height stage that ends at the buttons. Nav: P mark + PABLO left, five links right. Pablo's portrait centred, frontal, warm key light, filling the upper 55%. H1 "Webs y software a medida, hechos en Ibiza." centred in two lines over his chest, lead below, tally pill "Cuéntame tu proyecto" + text link "Ver proyectos". Nothing else above the fold.

FORM: Escenario de keynote, the model's pick (option 2 of the ordered list), steered by the owner with the assigned direction's palette (seed:e53a6c1fa3e3f5ef). Signature entrance "Mezcla": headline words rise large in the centre, settle into place, and the key light comes up on Pablo; then lead and actions arrive out of blur. Signature section: Vestta's real tour recording on a screen in perspective beside left-aligned copy, turning towards the visitor on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
